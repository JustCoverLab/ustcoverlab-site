NFL Player Impact Ratings

Snapshot from Nov 16
Points a team's rating drops if the player sits, as the NFL model values him. QBs are measured against a backup. Everyone else uses position base × snap share, plus a production bonus. 2026 season through week 10.
#PlayerTeamPosSnapsProductionImpact

Production: receiving yards (WR, TE), scrimmage yards (RB), pass rush (sacks ×2 + QB hits + tackles for loss) for DL and LB, and coverage (INT ×2 + passes defended) for CB and S, each vs the average of the top half of starters at the position (1.00 = typical good starter). For QBs it's the model's EPA per play. Linebackers get no pass-rush credit in the current model; amber notes show what a pass-rushing linebacker would be worth rated as an edge rusher. That is football value only: in our 2018-25 tests, betting lines did not move any more for these players, so the model keeps the lower line value. Only players with 40%+ of snaps are rated. Linemen have no production stat, so they get the position base.
