Green = edge at the estimated price, red = none. Tap a square to load it above. Look for where green starts: that's the fewest points (and cheapest price) worth taking.

How it works: for the game's spread and total, the calculator finds past NFL games with the favorite within 1 point of this spread and a total within 4 (wider if there are fewer than 250), and counts how often both legs would have won. Pushes are left out. The amber number takes 2 points off that win rate as a cushion for sample noise. The estimated price treats the two legs as independent with normal NFL scoring spread, then adds the markup; your -125 Commanders quote implies about 14%. Typing BetOnline's real price always overrides the estimate. Paper-testing tool, not betting advice.
